Section 01: Information We Collect

To deliver dynamic proximity matchmaking, live audio/video broadcast rooms, secure direct messaging, and interactive in-room gaming, LiveTogather collects the following categories of data:

  • Profile & Account Information: Display name, age, gender identity, dating intent and preferences ("Interested In"), occupation, education, city, location name, bio quote, lifestyle tags, zodiac sign, languages spoken, and communication habits.
  • Photos, Avatars & Moments Media: Profile pictures, luxury avatar frames, and user-submitted multimedia posts published to the Moments feed, including captions, likes count, and upload timestamps hosted on Google Cloud Storage.
  • 1-on-1 Direct Chats & Messaging: Message texts, transmission timestamps, delivery statuses, sender and recipient UIDs, and read receipts.
  • Real-Time Room Activity & Presence: Active presence marker, role (host, co-host, speaker, or listener), mute state, and synchronized background music queue inside Intent Rooms.
  • Geolocation Data (GPS): Latitude and longitude coordinates with explicit runtime consent to compute proximity distances to nearby members.
  • Device Identifiers & Hardware Telemetry: Hardware device identifiers (Android ID), device brand/model, OS version, and anonymous crash telemetry for fraud prevention and authentication.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Anonymous Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens for real-time alerts.

Section 02: Real-Time Audio & Video Streaming (Agora RTC)

  • Live Ephemeral Transmission: Voice conversations and video broadcasts inside Intent Rooms are transmitted dynamically through Agora RTC infrastructure in real-time.
  • No Unauthorized Server Recording: AYAD Creations does NOT continuously record, archive, or store live audio or video streams on servers. Sessions terminate immediately upon exit.
  • Cryptographic Token Protection: Room access is protected by dynamic, time-limited cryptographic tokens (RtcTokenBuilder2).

Section 03: Profile Verification & Anti-Catfishing Authenticity (CameraX & Audio)
